FAQ

Where are my tasks stored?
Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server and no account is needed.

I don't receive reminders.
Open Settings → Notifications → Daily Todo and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminders are only sent for tasks with a due time and the reminder switch turned on.

How do I delete a task?
Swipe the task to the left. You can tap "Undo" right after deleting.
